## About the references you collect

Most of them should **not** be websites. Websites as reference produce websites
that look like other websites. Photos of physical things — signage, posters,
packaging, notice boards, book covers, tickets — are where a look that belongs
to this project specifically will come from.

Ugly things you find interesting are more useful than beautiful things you feel
neutral about.
